# T002: Push required observation columns into the reader

[Task index](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

Read complete candidate signal sets and QC fields; avoid loading unused Doppler/other signals for TEC.

## Context

TEC needs complete candidate pairs and QC evidence. A smaller reader projection is useful only if it preserves alternate signals, raw quality flags and accepted measurements.

## Scope

`gnss_tec/tec/tec_calculation.py`, `gnss_tec/rinex/read_rinex.py`, `src/lib.rs`.

## Implementation Plan

1. Inspect current reader filtering and TEC candidate selection; enumerate all code/phase alternatives and required LLI/epoch/state/SNR fields for the supported modes.
2. Propose a reader projection derived from the complete candidate requirements, retaining deterministic fallback and all QC inputs. Keep the general raw-reader API's requested observation semantics intact.
3. Implement the narrow projection at the TEC-to-reader boundary and retain wide/long layout parity and diagnostics.
4. Compare against a matched unprojected reference, measuring accepted outputs, parse/pivot cost and isolated memory. Consult T001 or establish a local matched baseline.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] identical accepted measurements with lower measured memory and parse/pivot cost.
- [ ] Fallback candidates and QC/state metadata remain available in both layouts; the lower measured cost is attributable to the projection under matched inputs.

## Validation

Exercise missing preferred phases, invalid preferred phases, signal switches and retained diagnostics; compare accepted measurement identity/dtypes/nulls/numerics. Measure parse/pivot time and peak RSS on identical full inputs, not just fewer output columns.

## Progress & Handoff

**Next:** Inventory the current candidate/QC projection contract. The full projection and matched performance cases must be confirmed before implementation.
