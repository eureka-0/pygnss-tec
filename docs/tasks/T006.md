# T006: Stabilize and accelerate receiver-bias estimation

[Task index](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

Check epoch support, matrix rank/conditioning and solver status; center/scale features; precompute MSTD sufficient statistics; return uncertainty and failure reasons.
For 0.5.0, first prevent unsupported or unidentifiable estimates from being reported as successful calibration.

## Context

MSTD/LSQ estimation needs explicit support, rank, conditioning and failure contracts.
Unsupported or failed estimation must not become zero correction.

The 2026-10-18 review of `2370e03` reproduced false success with two satellites sharing the same mapping factor at each epoch.
With a known receiver bias of 5 TECU and VTEC of 20 TECU, MSTD returned approximately 500 TECU and VTEC of -416.8 TECU; LSQ returned approximately 8.003 TECU, and both reported successful estimation.
These are degenerate controls, not accuracy measurements for identifiable observations.

Receiver estimation can proceed independently of the T031/T032 reader-state round.
The delivered default continuity correction changes the final product reference; estimator validation must inspect the fitted bias and failure propagation before final alignment as well as public STEC/VTEC afterward.
Smooth final output alone cannot establish that a bias estimate is identifiable or calibrated correctly.

## Scope

`gnss_tec/calibration/receiver_bias.py`, failure propagation in `gnss_tec/calculation/tec.py`, `tests/calibration/test_bias.py`, `tests/calculation/test_reference.py` and affected calibration documentation.
Retain the existing scientific model and default output selection unless a contract change is reviewed.

## Implementation Plan

1. Turn the constant-mapping counterexample into a small permanent regression, alongside an identifiable known-bias control, single-SV and insufficient-epoch cases.
   Inspect fitted bias and failure propagation before final continuity alignment and public STEC/VTEC afterward.
2. Specify estimator-specific identifiability checks for review before implementation.
   For MSTD, inspect the within-epoch mapping-factor sensitivity to receiver bias and reject flat objectives; numerical optimizer success alone is insufficient.
   For LSQ, use a rank-revealing decomposition on appropriately scaled features to determine whether the receiver-bias coefficient is identifiable.
   Separate an unidentifiable receiver coefficient from redundant nuisance columns; do not reject an identifiable control merely because the full feature matrix lacks column rank.
   Review support/conditioning tolerances and boundary-solution behavior against independent controls rather than choosing arbitrary sample counts or a new prior.
3. Implement the reviewed failure checks using the existing failed-estimation contract: null receiver bias, `receiver_estimation_failed`, actionable reasons and null public TEC where required.
   Verify diagnostic projection and default-row behavior without treating failure as zero bias or bypassing it during final alignment.
4. Verify both methods under the supported dependency combinations and record the 0.5.0 safeguard outcome.
   This release safeguard can be accepted before the broader optimization and uncertainty work is complete.
5. Complete reviewed centering/scaling, uncertainty reporting and MSTD sufficient-statistic reuse; benchmark matched whole-day estimation without weakening the scientific checks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The 0.5.0 constant-mapping counterexample fails explicitly for both methods; independent identifiable controls recover the known bias and TEC.
- [ ] Recover identifiable known biases and reject controls with an unidentifiable receiver-bias coefficient, including degenerate single-SV, constant-mapping and insufficient-support cases.
- [ ] Unsupported or failed estimation retains an uncalibrated/null-TEC meaning; uncertainty and failure reasons are exposed under the reviewed result contract.
- [ ] Any scaling/statistics optimization preserves accepted outputs and records matched whole-day runtime and peak memory.

## Validation

Use full-day MSTD/LSQ references plus known-bias synthetic systems, single-SV, constant-mapping and insufficient-epoch cases. Inspect solver status, rank/condition behavior, residuals and reported uncertainty; measure speed without weakening physical assertions.
Include redundant nuisance features, near-degenerate geometry, nonfinite input and optimizer failure, checking bias/status/null masks as well as final TEC.
Store durable controls in the regression suite; temporary local reports are not task dependencies.
