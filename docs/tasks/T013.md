# T013: Normalize DSB/OSB products and add product retrieval

[Task index](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

Support reverse pairs, validated bias chains and OSB differences; retrieve supported IGS/CAS/CODE/GFZ products with provenance, checksums, retries and offline caching.

## Context

DSB/OSB normalization and retrieval need explicit sign, validity and datum rules. Widening accepted bias semantics must be deliberate.

## Scope

`gnss_tec/tec/bias.py`, new product I/O/cache modules.

## Implementation Plan

1. Inventory the current bias parser/normalization contract and candidate IGS/CAS/CODE/GFZ products, including datum, units, time scale, validity and provenance.
2. Propose canonical reverse-pair signs, validated bias chains and OSB differencing rules with overlap/datum rejection. Obtain review before widening accepted scientific semantics.
3. Implement normalization with independent numerical fixtures, then add retrieval/checksum/retry/offline-cache behavior as a separate validated step.
4. Verify reproducible product selection from an explicit manifest, online and cached/offline paths, overlap rejection and missing/unsupported product behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] reproducible product selection with no silent overlap or datum mixing.
- [ ] Validity intervals, sign, units/time scale and datum remain traceable; retrieval failures or cache conflicts cannot silently substitute a different product.

## Validation

Use independent DSB/OSB/reverse-chain cases and supported product samples with checksums/provenance. Test overlap, mixed datum, expiry, retrieval failure, retries and offline reproduction; preserve existing correct DSB sign.

## Progress & Handoff

**Next:** Inventory supported product semantics and draft canonical normalization/datum rules. New providers or OSB support are not yet validated.
